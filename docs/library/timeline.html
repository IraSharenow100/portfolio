History / Timeline

Date Event Source
1948 or 1949 [conflicting sources] Original El Cerrito Library constructed at 6510 Stockton Avenue. One source (the Feb. 2026 Impact Report) states 1948; a separate September 2026 Task Force presentation states 1949. Not yet resolved. Impact Report, 2/19/2026, p.3; Sept. 8, 2026 Task Force Presentation, p.13
1960 Library expanded to ~6,500 sq ft. Impact Report, p.3
2002 City begins actively evaluating sites for a new library (Portola Middle School site, Civic Theater site, Community Center, San Pablo Ave sites, current site). Impact Report, p.3–4; Agenda Bill 8.B, 1/20/2026
2006 City retains independent experts; first comprehensive Library Needs Assessment + Building Program completed, sized at ~21,000 sq ft, prepared to pursue a state construction bond grant. Impact Report, p.3
2006 (Nov.) Statewide bond measure that would have funded the grant program fails at the ballot. Impact Report, p.3
2013 City Council adopts Strategic Plan identifying the library as a priority. Impact Report, p.3; Nov. 21, 2023 presentation timeline slide
2014 (March) City retains Kathryn Page Associates; updated Needs Assessment presented to Council, recommending ~20,845 sq ft. Impact Report, p.3
2015 Strategic Plan updated; site options and cost models developed for a possible ballot measure. Impact Report, p.4; Nov. 21, 2023 timeline slide
June 2, 2015 Godbe Research poll reported to Council: 72% of respondents favorable to a $30M library bond (40.9% “definitely yes,” 30.6% “probably yes”); poll of 418 online + 203 phone respondents in March 2015. Up from 69% support in a 2013 poll. Times Herald, “El Cerrito poll finds strong support for new library,” 6/4/2015
July 19, 2016 City Council unanimously votes to place a $30 million library bond measure (Measure B) on the November 2016 ballot; also authorizes MOU with BART (Resolution 2016-68) to study TOD/library feasibility at the Plaza Station. MOU BART library cc1608167.pdf; Impact Report p.4; Agenda Bill 8.A p.2
July 12, 2016 City’s Financial Advisory Board votes 3-1 to recommend ballot language explicitly disclose the measure as “a tax on real estate”; recommendation is not incorporated into the final ballot language adopted July 19. East Bay Times editorial, 10/9/2016
Aug. 16, 2016 Agenda Bill formalizing MOU with BART for TOD/library feasibility study presented to Council. MOU BART library cc1608167.pdf
Nov. 8, 2016 Measure B (2016): $30M library bond. Required 66.67% supermajority. Received 63% “Yes” (Impact Report) — reported elsewhere as 64% by a public commenter [conflicting figures in the record]. Fell short by ~500 votes. Impact Report, p.4; public comment of Georgina Edwards, 11/21/2023 packet
2018 AB 2923 takes effect, requiring minimum zoning on BART-owned parcels. Nov. 21, 2023 timeline slide
2018 MOU between City and BART (authorized 2016) formally executed. Impact Report, p.29; Agenda Bill 8.A
2018–2019 El Cerrito Plaza Existing Conditions Report, BART Access Survey, and Parking Management Case Studies prepared. Impact Report, p.29
Nov. 19, 2019 City Council adopts El Cerrito Plaza TOD Goals & Objectives, including “exploring options for integrating a public library into the TOD”; also requests (Resolution 2019-66) that BART include a potential library in its RFQ. Impact Report, p.29; Agenda Bill 8.A; Nov. 21, 2023 timeline slide
2019 Library Workshop held to discuss proposed sites & partnerships. Nov. 21, 2023 timeline slide
July 2020 BART initiates RFQ process, inviting developers to submit two conceptual site plans — one with a library, one without. Impact Report, p.29
Nov. 2020 Developer team of Related California, Holliday Development, and SAHA selected; BART enters Exclusive Negotiating Agreement. Impact Report, p.29; Nov. 21, 2023 timeline slide
2020 BART adopts El Cerrito Plaza TOD Goals & Objectives (later amended 2021). Agenda Bill 8.A
2021–2023 TOD pre-planning: access planning, public engagement, library-specific public meetings, site planning. Nov. 21, 2023 timeline slide
Oct. 3, 2022 Developer submits SB 330/AB 2923 application for the first TOD parcel, ahead of the SPASP update. Agenda Bill 8.A
Dec. 6, 2022 San Pablo Avenue Specific Plan (SPASP) updated. Agenda Bill 8.A
Dec. 2023 City’s Strategic Plan 2024–2029 adopted, articulating a goal to “Create a community hub anchored by the Plaza Transit-Oriented Development and a new Library.” Agenda Bill 8.B, 1/20/2026
May 2023 Godbe Research poll (Tier 1 messaging ~59–60% support depending on statement). Ira Sharenow letter, 11/19/2023, citing “May 2023 Survey: 60% YES; 34% NO”
Sept./Oct. 2023 Second Godbe poll round; full support figures not publicly disclosed per a CPRA request (see Cost & Funding History). Ira Sharenow letter to City Clerk, 11/19/2023
Sept. 27, 2023 El Cerrito Library Public Forum held. el-cerrito.org/library timeline
Nov. 21, 2023 Council study session (Item 9B) on the Plaza TOD library option; presentation identifies the library site as “Phase 4” of the TOD master plan; Council opts to explore a 2025 (rather than March 2024) ballot measure. Public comment from Al Miller, Michael Fischer, Georgina Edwards, Greg Lyman, Charles Taylor, and Ira Sharenow. November 21 2023 presentation.pdf
2024–2025 Site plan finalized: library located on ground floor of a future affordable-housing building at Fairmount Ave & Liberty St, “Parcel C West.” Half-acre public plaza and potential café also part of TOD Master Plan. Impact Report, p.29–30
June 11, 2024 City holds a Community Library Workshop at the El Cerrito Library. el-cerrito.org/library timeline
April 1, 2025 City executes Consulting Services Agreement with Griffin Structures (Owner’s Representative) for Library Project services. Griffin Structures Inc. Agreement, 2025.06.30, p.2–3
May 6, 2025 Citizen proponents file a Notice of Intent to Circulate an Initiative Petition (“The El Cerrito Library Initiative”) with the City Clerk. Agenda Bill 8.A, 1/20/2026, p.1; elcerrito.gov/1753
May 21, 2025 City Attorney (Sky Woodruff) provides the Ballot Title and Summary, starting the 180-day circulation clock. Agenda Bill 8.A, p.1
June 13–17, 2025 Proponents file a revised measure text; City Attorney issues a revised Ballot Title and Summary on June 17, 2025, restarting the 180-day circulation period. Agenda Bill 8.A, p.1
June 27, 2025 Griffin Structures proposal for Owner’s Rep services through Sept. 2025, including DA negotiation support and Council presentation prep. Griffin Structures Inc. 2025.06.30.1.pdf
Nov. 20, 2025 Signed petitions submitted to City Clerk (2,454 signatures per the Clerk’s prima facie count, prior to verification). Agenda Bill 8.A, p.1; Certificate of Petition
Dec. 1, 2025 Petition deemed “filed” following City Clerk’s Prima Facie review. Agenda Bill 8.A, p.1
Dec. 16, 2025 Council installs Gabe Quinto as Mayor for 2026 (AG Rob Bonta administers oath); library tax measure discussed informally at the reception. ECCRG blog, referencing 12/16/2025 meeting
Jan. 9, 2026 Contra Costa County Elections confirms the petition contains sufficient valid signatures — 2,177 qualifying signatures after verification. Agenda Bill 8.A, p.1; City Clerk’s Certificate of Petition; Sept. 8, 2026 Task Force Presentation
Jan. 20, 2026 Council adopts Resolution 2026-04, accepting the Certificate of Petition; Council requests a §9212 Impact Report and holds a study session (Item 8.B) on the current facility and five conceptual scenarios ($10M–$48M range). Impact Report, p.2; Agenda Bill 8.A & 8.B, 1/20/2026
Feb. 6, 2026 Strategic Growth Council (SGC) staff (Justin DeWaele) advise, in an email later forwarded to Council, that AHSC Round 10 is likely to open March 2026 with a May/June 2026 deadline — undercutting the stated rationale that a June election would help a Parcel C West AHSC application. SGC letter forwarded to council.docx
Feb. 17, 2026 Council adopts Resolution 2026-11, formally creating the El Cerrito Library Task Force (advisory; 7 voting residents incl. 1 youth member, 1-year terms, plus 3 non-voting invitees). elcerrito.gov/1768/Library-Task-Force
Feb. 19, 2026 Special Council meeting: staff present the §9212 Impact Report; Council votes 4-1 (Ktsanes dissenting) to call a special election for June 2, 2026, adopting Resolution 2026-12. Wysinger urges the Council to stop “analysis and paralysis”; Ktsanes argues November would be cheaper/more democratic; Quinto confirms election costs would be reimbursed if the measure passes. Impact Report, p.2; Contra Costa News, 3/10/2026; elcerrito.gov/1753
March 9, 2026 Contra Costa Registrar of Voters randomly assigns ballot letters; the El Cerrito measure becomes Measure C. elcerrito.gov/1753
March 12, 2026 City Attorney Sky Woodruff signs the official Impartial Analysis of Measure C. contracostavote.gov/m9002-en
March 18, 2026 Deadline for primary ballot arguments (for/against). elcerrito.gov/1753; Contra Costa News, 3/10/2026
March 23, 2026 Deadline for rebuttal arguments. Same as above
April 7, 2026 Council holds a study session on proposed exemptions to Measure C’s parcel tax; directs staff to return with a resolution clarifying/loosening exemption qualification requirements. el-cerrito.org/library timeline
May 4, 2026 Vote-by-mail ballots mailed. ca-elcerrito2.civicplus.com/217/Elections
June 2, 2026 ELECTION DAY. Measure C on the ballot as part of the California Statewide Direct Primary Election. Multiple sources
June 2, 2026 (result) Measure C failed. YES 3,264 (30.39%) — NO 7,475 (69.61%) of 10,739 total votes. Turnout 62.77%. See Measure C for the certified Statement of Results. Certificate of the City of El Cerrito, City Clerk, dated July 21, 2026
August 2026 City states publicly that no dedicated library funding has been identified. City’s library webpage, elcerrito.gov/library
Sept. 8, 2026 First Library Task Force meeting scheduled, 6:00 p.m., City Council Chambers — presents five post-election alternatives (renovate, renovate/expand, Plaza TOD, new standalone site, renovate a purchased building) since no dedicated funding source has been identified. Sept. 8, 2026 Task Force Agenda, Presentation
2028 (projected) Construction of “Phase 6” (the building slated to house the library, if funded) is not expected to begin before 2028 per the City’s TOD phasing information; City must execute a Development Agreement with the TOD developer by March 2027. el-cerrito.org/library; Impact Report, p.32–33

Note on TOD phase numbering: the library building’s phase number in the TOD master plan has changed more than once across City documents — “Phase 4” in the Nov. 2023 presentation and the Feb. 2026 SGC letter, “Phase 6” on the City’s current library webpage. This reflects the TOD master plan being renumbered multiple times, not a single discrepancy to resolve.
