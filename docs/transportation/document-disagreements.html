Where City Documents Disagree

Last updated September 20, 2026.

City, state and agency documents about these projects give different facts, dates or numbers for the same thing in a number of places. This page lists the main ones with both sources. A disagreement is not a finding of error. Many come from different dates, definitions or study areas. Where a difference is explained, the table says so. The full list (50 entries) is in the download at the bottom of the page.

Homes, plans and rules

Homes entitled 1,500 by 2018 (Implementation deck, slide 4) “Over 1,500” as of 2022 (Specific Plan Chapter 1)
Inclusionary threshold Rental buildings of 9 or more homes (City Specific Plan page) 10 or more (Housing deck, slide 13)
Specific Plan goals “Six goals,” five listed (City page) Four goals in the 2022 update; five (A–E) in 2014 (Chapter 1)
Priority Development Areas “Three PDAs” (City page) One PDA designated in 2007; Downtown, Midtown and Uptown are focus areas (Chapter 1)
Housing Element Plan adopted “to advance” the 2023–2031 element (City page) Earlier element was certified May 2015; 2023–2031 element adopted August 15, 2023
Hana Gardens 63 homes (Housing deck, slide 11) 62 homes (fund report; May 6, 2025 agenda bill); finished 2018 (bill) or 2019 (fund report)
