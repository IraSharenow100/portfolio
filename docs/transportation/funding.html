Who Pays: Funding, Consultants and Staff

Last updated September 20, 2026.

Nearly every project on these pages depends on grants from state, federal, county and regional agencies rather than the City’s own funds. This page lists the main sources, the City’s housing fund, and the consultants and City staff named in the documents.

How to read dollar figures

  • Read every figure with its date. The same project has different totals in different years.
  • An award is money promised, not money spent. An allocation (bonds, tax credits) is financing authority, not cash.
  • Do not add a project total to the grants inside it. That counts the same money twice.
  • The complete list of figures, with the document and page behind each, is in the funding ledger (download below).

The City’s own housing fund

At June 30, 2025 the Housing Successor fund held $6,960,307 on paper. Only $105,410 was available cash. The rest is loans and interest the fund is owed. See El Cerrito Plaza BART Housing and RDA Dissolution.

Source: SB 341 Housing Successor Report FY2024/25 (Sections III and XII), attached to Agenda Bill 6.D, March 17, 2026.
